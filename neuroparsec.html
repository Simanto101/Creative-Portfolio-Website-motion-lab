NeuroParsec

AI for clinical care
and medical education.

NeuroParsec is my HealthTech company — built from first principles to change how care is delivered, starting with the tools clinicians and students actually use.

The mission

Technology that heals.

Healthcare runs on knowledge — and knowledge should be instant, visual and trustworthy. NeuroParsec builds AI systems that bring clinical understanding to the point of care and the classroom.

No gimmicks. Real tools, engineered carefully, tested in the real world.

What we're building

In the lab.

02

AI assistants that help clinicians reason faster — decision support, not decision replacement.

03

Med Education

Learning experiences that make complex medicine intuitive — built with educators, for students.
